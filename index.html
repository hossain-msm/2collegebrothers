What the referral sources reveal

AI discovery is not limited to one platform.

The latest year shows a broader mix of AI referrals. ChatGPT still dominates volume, while Gemini, Claude and Perplexity are contributing more sessions than in the preceding period.

ChatGPT drives the largest share of recorded activity.

Of the 392 website key events reported for AI-referred visits in the latest period, 379 were attributed to ChatGPT. That makes it the clearest near-term source to examine when connecting referral visits to verified inquiries.

Emerging platforms add another layer of visibility.

Gemini increased from 8 to 64 sessions, Claude from 6 to 55, and Perplexity from 11 to 42. Those are small absolute volumes today—but they show why businesses should monitor more than one AI discovery source.

More platforms. More ways to be discovered. One consistent standard for measurement.

Referral-source reporting indicates that a visitor reached the website from an AI platform. It does not prove that the brand was explicitly recommended in every visit, and key events should not be interpreted as confirmed leads.

Straight answers

Questions moving company owners ask.

Does 147% more AI traffic mean 147% more customers?

No. It means more website sessions referred by tracked AI platforms. We measure qualified leads and booked revenue separately.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my moving company?

No agency can guarantee placement or a recommendation in a third-party AI platform. The goal is to improve your discoverability and measure actual visibility and referral outcomes over time.

Do I need to stop investing in Google SEO?

No. AI Optimization complements traditional SEO, local search and other customer acquisition work. Your business needs to be discoverable wherever relevant customers research movers.

What will an AI visibility review tell me?

We'll discuss your markets, current visibility goals, relevant service pages and the data available to identify opportunities for your moving company. Specific scope is confirmed during the conversation.

Does this case study prove MSM caused every increase?

No. The data shows observed growth during an ongoing client relationship. AI adoption, changing user behavior and other marketing factors can influence traffic. We present the evidence without assigning unsupported exclusive causation.
